title: Desk Widgets — Privacy policy
---


*Last updated: November 7, 2026*

Desk Widgets shows clocks and the weather on your Windows desktop. It is free, has no account or sign-in, shows no ads and does not track you.

## What leaves your computer

**The weather forecast.** To show the weather, Desk Widgets asks [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/) for the forecast at each place your weather widgets show. A request contains only the place's coordinates, rounded to about 1 km, and the usual technical details any internet request carries (such as your IP address). This happens about every 30 minutes per place while the widgets are running, and not while your PC is locked or offline.

**City search.** When you type in a "Search for a city" box, the text you type is sent to Open-Meteo's place search to find matching cities.

**"Use my location".** Only if you press this button, Desk Widgets asks Windows for your location (Windows asks for your permission first). The coordinates are used for the forecast as above, and sent once to [OpenStreetMap Nominatim](https://nominatim.openstreetmap.org/) to find the name of the place. Your location is not used otherwise and is not shared with anyone else.

Open-Meteo and OpenStreetMap have their own privacy policies: [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms#privacy), [OpenStreetMap Foundation](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy).

**If the app crashes.** Windows may report the crash to Microsoft, according to your Windows diagnostic data settings. Through the Microsoft Store we see only anonymous totals (how often a version crashed, and where in the code), never who you are.

## What stays on your computer

Your settings (widgets, their places and time zones, positions and look) and the last forecasts are saved in the app's own folder on your PC, so the widgets work offline. A small log file records errors to help fix problems; it is never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

## What we don't do

No account, no personal data collected by us, no analytics, no advertising, no selling or sharing of data. We have no servers: the app talks only to the two services named above (and opens open-meteo.com in your browser if you click its credit).

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
